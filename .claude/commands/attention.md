# attention

Run `node scripts/auction.mjs attention --json`. Group by category: buyers overdue, settlements not run, overdue or waiting on bank details, lots not collected, lots passed in and still here, lots in the store and not in a sale, lots in the next sale with no reserve, artist resale royalties, taonga tūturu, cash, unsigned consignment agreements, vendor details. Lead with anything that breaks the law or a vendor's terms (a late royalty report, a taonga with no certificate or going to a buyer who is not a registered collector, cash over the limit, a settlement past its date), then money owed to us, then the store. One line each, with the record reference.
